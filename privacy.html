Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Omarmail is a personal desktop email client, a private instance of the open-source Omamail plugin for Omarchy. It is operated by imwsl (yangjie6020@gmail.com) for their own use.

2. Data we access

When you sign in with Google, Omarmail requests these OAuth scopes and accesses the data they cover:

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): your email messages (headers, bodies and attachments), labels, and your Gmail address.
  • Gmail (gmail.send): the ability to send messages you write.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): your calendar events.

3. How we use it

Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app: listing, reading, searching, labeling, archiving, trashing and sending mail; showing and answering meeting invitations; and showing and editing calendar events. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and Omarmail does not use it to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Where it is stored

Everything stays on your own computer. There is no Omarmail server.

  • Refresh token: in the system keyring (GNOME Keyring / Secret Service).
  • Access token: in memory only, never written to disk.
  • OAuth client ID and secret: ~/.config/omamail/credentials.json, readable only by your user account.
  • Cache (message lists, labels, profile, and up to 1,000 recently opened message bodies): ~/.cache/omamail, readable only by your user account.

Data moves only between your computer and Google's APIs, over HTTPS.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone, with these exceptions:

  • AI assistance (optional, user-initiated). Only when you explicitly ask for AI assistance (the AI button or Alt+G), the messages you selected (at most 20) or the draft you are writing, together with your question, are passed to the AI service configured as the default on your own computer — currently Anthropic's Claude, through its command-line tool and your own account. That provider processes the content under its own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent to an AI service automatically. Conversation history is kept locally in a private folder (at most 32 turns).
  • Legal requirements, if disclosure is required by law.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Omarmail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your computer until you delete it. Deleting it does not affect mail stored by Google.
  • Revoke Omarmail's access at any time: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local data: remove ~/.config/omamail and ~/.cache/omamail, and delete the Omamail entry from the system keyring.

8. Security

Tokens are kept in the system keyring or in memory; credential and cache files are readable only by your user account; all network traffic uses HTTPS. Remote images in messages are blocked until you choose to load them, so opening a message does not notify the sender.

9. Children

Omarmail is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 11 日

1. 我们是谁

Omarmail 是一个自用的桌面邮件客户端，是 Omarchy 开源插件 Omamail 的个人实例，由 imwsl （yangjie6020@gmail.com）运营，供其本人使用。

2. 访问的数据

使用 Google 账号登录时，Omarmail 申请以下 OAuth 权限范围，并访问其涵盖的数据：

  • Gmail（gmail.modify）：你的邮件（邮件头、正文和附件）、标签以及 Gmail 地址。
  • Gmail（gmail.send）：发送你撰写的邮件。
  • Google 日历（calendar.events）：你的日历事件。

3. 用途

Google 用户数据仅用于提供你在应用中看到的功能：列出、阅读、搜索、标记、归档、移入垃圾箱和发送邮件；显示并回复会议邀请；显示并编辑日历事件。这些数据不用于广告，不出售，Omarmail 也不会用它来训练人工智能或机器学习模型。

4. 存储位置

所有数据都保存在你自己的电脑上，Omarmail 没有服务器。

  • 刷新令牌：保存在系统钥匙串（GNOME Keyring / Secret Service）中。
  • 访问令牌：只在内存中，从不写入磁盘。
  • OAuth 客户端 ID 与密钥：~/.config/omamail/credentials.json，仅你的用户账户可读。
  • 缓存（邮件列表、标签、个人资料，以及最多 1000 封最近打开的邮件正文）：~/.cache/omamail，仅你的用户账户可读。

数据只通过 HTTPS 在你的电脑和 Google API 之间传输。

5. 共享

我们不会向任何人共享、出售或转让 Google 用户数据，以下情况除外：

  • AI 辅助（可选，由用户主动发起）。只有当你明确请求 AI 辅助（点击 AI 按钮或按 Alt+G）时，你选中的邮件（最多 20 封）或正在撰写的草稿，连同你的问题，才会交给你电脑上设为默认的 AI 服务——目前是 Anthropic 的 Claude，通过其命令行工具、使用你自己的账号。该服务按其自身的条款和隐私政策处理这些内容。不会自动向任何 AI 服务发送内容。对话记录保存在本地的私有目录中（最多 32 轮）。
  • 法律要求：法律要求披露时。

6. Google API 服务用户数据政策

Omarmail 对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用及向其他应用的传输，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”要求。

7. 保留与删除

  • 本地数据会一直保留在你的电脑上，直到你删除为止。删除本地数据不影响 Google 服务器上保存的邮件。
  • 可随时撤销 Omarmail 的访问权限：myaccount.google.com/permissions。
  • 删除本地数据：删除 ~/.config/omamail 和 ~/.cache/omamail，并从系统钥匙串中删除 Omamail 的条目。

8. 安全

令牌保存在系统钥匙串或内存中；凭据和缓存文件仅你的用户账户可读；所有网络通信均使用 HTTPS。邮件中的远程图片默认不加载，需要你手动选择，因此打开邮件不会通知发件人。

9. 儿童

Omarmail 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集他们的数据。

10. 变更

本政策如有变更，新版本将发布在本页面并更新生效日期。
